Jelly Roomies Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Jelly Roomies is published by jacdoesit.

We collect anonymous gameplay and error data to improve the game. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

  • The game has no accounts and no advertising.
  • We use GameAnalytics to understand how the game is played. It receives level starts and completions, moves, time per level, hint use, tutorial progress, error and crash reports, device type, operating system, language, approximate country, and an app-specific device identifier.
  • We do not ask for your advertising identifier and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • You can turn this off at any time: open Pause in the game and set Analytics to Off.
  • Your progress and settings (current level, furthest level reached, coin balance and hint state, sound, music and reduced-motion preferences) are saved only on your device, in the game's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other device data.
  • The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address before the change takes effect.
